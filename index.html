Personal Rclone Drive

This is a personal Google Drive integration using rclone, a command-line file management tool.

Its OAuth client allows the account owner to authorize rclone to access Google Drive files for operations such as listing, copying, syncing, creating, changing, or deleting files as directed by the owner. The permissions requested depend on the rclone configuration.

For information about data access and handling, see the Privacy Policy.
