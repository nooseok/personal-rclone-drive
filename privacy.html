Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Personal Rclone Drive is a personal OAuth client used with rclone to access the account owner's Google Drive. It is intended for the owner's use.

Data accessed and use

Depending on the permissions granted and commands run, rclone can access file names, metadata and file contents, and create, modify or delete files in Google Drive. This access is used to perform the file operations the owner requests, such as copying, synchronizing or listing files.

Storage and sharing

rclone runs on a device or server selected by the owner. Its configuration may contain OAuth tokens, and files or logs may be stored on that machine or at destinations selected by the owner. File operations may transfer data between Google Drive and those destinations. Google and any other selected storage provider process data under their own policies.

Retention and control

Data and tokens remain in locations selected by the owner until removed, subject to the retention settings of those locations and providers. The owner can revoke the app's Google account access through Google Account connections and remove local rclone configuration or copies as appropriate.

Contact

For questions, contact the owner through the GitHub profile.
